ABOUT

About Gen 3 ACE Archive

Gen 3 ACE Archive is an unofficial fan project by MankeyMite and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc., or The Pokémon Company.

Purpose of the archive

Gen 3 ACE Archive aims to be a public repository of ACE codes and guides collected from across the ACE community and other websites. Its goal is to provide a user-friendly place where players, especially beginners, can find codes compatible with their game and learn the fundamentals of how ACE works through approachable explanations.

Questions and community

For questions about ACE, feel free to join MankeyMite's Discord server or visit the YouTube channel.
